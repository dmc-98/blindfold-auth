# Code of Conduct

Blindfold Auth is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- being respectful in language and tone
- assuming good intent while still giving clear technical feedback
- welcoming newcomers and helping them get unblocked
- accepting accountability and apologizing when harm is caused
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and process rather than people

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption
- doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention
- threats of violence or encouraging harmful behavior

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, chats, and other public or private interactions connected to the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from the project community.
